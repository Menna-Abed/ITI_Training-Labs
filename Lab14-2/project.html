Easy Homemade Chocolate Cake

A quick, rich, and delicious recipe for every dessert lover!

Ingredients Nutrition Instructions Video Guide

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup fresh milk

Nutrition Facts

Nutrient Amount
Calories 350 kcal
Fat 12g
Carbs 50g

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350oF (175oC) and grease a cake pan.
  2. Mix all dry ingredients in a large bowl.
  3. Add eggs and milk, then stir well until smooth.
  4. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until fully cooked.
